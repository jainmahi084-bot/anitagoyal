My Work & Impact

Menstrual Health & Women's Well-being

Personal guidance. Institutional education. Talking taboos.

As an MHM Coach & Educator, I support women from puberty to menopause through personal consultations and informed guidance on menstrual health and reusable products—cups, cloth pads and period panties.

My Professional Engagements

  • Personal Consultations: Individual guidance for informed menstrual health choices.
  • Schools: Age-appropriate sessions aligned with NEP.
  • Colleges & Institutions: Customised programmes tailored to institutional needs.
  • Corporates, NGOs & CSR: SDG-Aligned Programmes. Customised menstrual health education and community outreach aligned with relevant Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly SDG 3: Good Health & Well-being, SDG 5: Gender Equality and SDG 6: Clean Water & Sanitation.

Selected Engagements: MNIT college , B Lal Biotechnology college, Mansa College, Sun India school, Kangaroo Kids School, SVM School, Little Millennium School, Building Brains School, Sanskar School, MHM Summit 2025, JCERC college & ISSRF confrence.

Community Building | Jaipur Mommies

A decade of building communities, inspiring conscious living and empowering women.

Jaipur Mommies began as a conscious support community for mothers and has evolved into a network nurturing peer support, mindful choices, women’s growth and community engagement.

What makes Jaipur Mommies different?

  • 24-Hour Support Community: Peer support for mothers of children aged 0–14, across different stages of motherhood.
  • Pioneering Preloved Initiatives: Encouraging conscious consumption by extending the life of products. Bringing diff concepts groups to Jaipur which is followed by others like preloved group.
  • Women’s Reading Circle: Helping mothers reconnect with reading, personal growth and identities beyond motherhood.
  • Women Entrepreneur Support: Creating visibility and collaboration opportunities for homegrown women-led businesses.
  • Philanthropic & Environmental Initiatives: Community-led campaigns involving book and water bottle distribution, clothing donations and mango seed collection etc.
  • Brand & Event Collaborations: Connecting relevant brands, events and initiatives with an engaged community through meaningful partnerships.

Beyond Community Building

Introducing new concepts, encouraging conscious choices and bringing people together to create social and environmental impact.

CONSCIOUS LIVING | NaturEcoIndia

Sustainable Bulk Gifting | Everyday Foot Wellness

Founded by Anita Goyal, NaturEcoIndia has been bringing conscious choices into everyday living for 6+ years, through sustainable bulk gifting and mindful foot-wellness products.

What We Stand For

  • Conscious Festive & Corporate Gifting: Thoughtfully curated gifting for festivals, weddings, and corporate occasions, celebrating Jaipur’s local artisans and handmade craftsmanship.
  • Everyday Foot Wellness: Bringing attention to an often-neglected part of self-care. Our Foot Salve bridges the gap between petroleum-based products and expensive spa treatments, making foot care a simple, at-home ritual.

Our Purpose

Promoting conscious consumption, celebrating local craftsmanship and making everyday wellness more mindful.

Values & Personal Philosophy

I’m deeply rooted in Jaipur’s heritage and culture, with a deep love for the city and its traditions. A saree advocate, I see this timeless Indian attire as both a cultural expression and a sustainable choice.

I believe in rituals and traditions, but coming from a Baniya family, I also believe in questioning some of them through rational thinking rather than following them blindly.

Curiosity and continuous learning drive me. I’m always willing to learn, unlearn, and evolve.

I’m not the most consistent person on social media, but my work finds appreciation in the community I serve. For me, impact matters more than visibility.

At the heart of everything I do is one belief: if we want to equip the next generation to build a better future, we must first empower the mothers raising that generation.
